=== RENT MANAGER PRODUCT FEATURES ===

These are the core Rent Manager product features discussed at RMUC.26 sessions and available for demonstration at the Rent Manager Experience in Innovation Hall.

--- ACCOUNTING ---
Rent Manager includes a complete double-entry accounting system that works in both cash and accrual accounting simultaneously. Key capabilities include:

Accounts Receivable (A/R): Automate your monthly A/R cycle with A/R Automation — deliver delinquency notices via email, text, phone broadcast, or Virtual Post Office on a preset schedule. Create Charge Types (rent, late fees, parking, storage, etc.), receive and record payments, and make bank deposits directly in Rent Manager.

Accounts Payable (A/P): Create and pay one-time or recurring vendor bills, write and print checks directly from the software, manage credit card registers, and access full vendor contact and transaction history. AvidXchange integration enables no-cost digital vendor payments via AvidPay and AI-powered invoice scanning via AvidInvoice.

Payments: Process digital payments via ePay (credit/debit cards), CashPay (cash at 30,000+ CheckFreePay locations), Check Scanning, Lockbox (powered by Zego), eChecks, and no-cost vendor payments via AvidPay.

AI-Powered AR/AP Automation (new features): Bank Sync — securely connect bank and credit card accounts for AI-assisted reconciliation. Smart Bills — automatically capture invoice data and create payables. Smart Receipts — snap a photo of a receipt to auto-create a payable. Smart Check Scanning — automatically reads handwritten check details to minimize errors.

Electronic Bank Reconciliations: Reconcile bank and credit card accounts electronically using imported bank statements.

Financial Reporting: Generate Balance Sheet, General Ledger, Profit & Loss, Aged Receivables, Charge Detail, and Delinquency Reports in seconds.

Tax Tools: Generate vendor and owner 1099s, support electronic filing through the IRS FIRE system, and manage year-end close processes.

Budgeting: Track income and expenses against projected budgets, set budget warnings on financial transactions, and generate budget analysis reports.

Purchase Orders: Create, approve, and track purchase orders linked to bills and invoices, with status tracking (pending/approved/denied) and inventory quantity updates.

Loan Manager: Track thousands of owner-financed loans within Rent Manager, charge principal and interest like rent, estimate monthly payments, and calculate loan payoff amounts.

Utility Billing: Enter meter readings for gas, water, electric, and sewage each billing period and charge tenants directly within the software. See also: Metered Utilities in the Maintenance section.

Commercial Module: Manage commercial charges, CAM reconciliations, and produce commercial tenant/owner/vendor statements.

--- REPORTING ---
Rent Manager includes 450+ pre-built reports plus custom report-building tools:

Built-In Reports (450+): Includes Audit Trail, Balance Sheet, General Ledger, Profit & Loss, Aged Receivables, Budget reports, Prospect Box Score, Summary Rent Roll, Vacancy Report, Tax Forms (1099s), and state-specific forms like the Minnesota CRP.

Report Writer: Drag-and-drop custom report builder for property, tenant, prospect, owner, vendor, and unit reports. Includes pre-defined functions and variables specific to Rent Manager.

Financial Report Writer: Create or modify financial reports from scratch in any format, full of accounting data tailored to your business needs.

Report Automation (Rent Manager Online feature): Automatically generate and deliver scheduled report batches via email. Run multiple reports simultaneously with one click. Administrators can monitor delivery history and receive alerts if a batch fails.

Custom Reports: Rent Manager's Professional Services team can build reports entirely from scratch based on your specific requirements.

--- MAINTENANCE ---
Rent Manager manages the full maintenance lifecycle from issue creation to resolution:

Service Issues: Create, track, assign, and resolve maintenance tasks with status tracking (New, Work in Progress, Resolved, etc.). Memorize recurring issue templates for one-click creation. Field techs using rmAppSuite Pro can update issues in real time with GPS check-ins and tenant electronic sign-offs.

Maintenance Scheduling: Assign technicians, manage availability and working hours, group properties and techs, and let residents self-schedule service requests via Tenant Web Access (TWA) or the rmResident app. Automated notifications keep everyone updated when requests are created, scheduled, confirmed, and closed.

Issue Checklists: Add step-by-step checklists to service issues, mark steps complete, and advance issues to the next user in the workflow.

Service Tech Map: View real-time GPS locations of all rmAppSuite Pro field techs on a map, updated when techs check in to service issues.

Work Orders & Integrated Billing: Attach billable expenses directly to work orders and charge back to tenants or owners via invoices, with optional markup amounts.

Inspections: Conduct customizable inspections from the rmAppSuite Pro app — capture notes, photos, and videos; use reusable templates; create service issues directly from inspection findings.

Make Ready Boards: Visual board showing units in the make-ready process with color-coded status for service issues and inspections. Simplifies unit turnover and task delegation.

Metered Utilities: Enter meter readings (gas, water, sewer, electric) via scanner, mobile device, or import. Post utility charges to tenants, track consumption history, and handle complex billing with Metered Utilities Plus (for Texas, California, Michigan, Arizona and other states requiring special calculations).

Project Management: Convert multifaceted tasks like move-ins and move-outs into clear, delegated steps for your team.

=== ABOUT RENT MANAGER & LCS ===

Rent Manager takes data security seriously and maintains multiple layers of protection:

Granular Access Controls: Comprehensive permissions system lets administrators customize user access down to the individual property level. Enforces the principle of least privilege.

Multifactor Authentication (MFA): Requires two forms of verification at login to prevent unauthorized database access.

PCI Level 1 Compliance: Electronic payments are processed through a PCI Level 1 compliant system via Zego — the highest level of payment security compliance.

SOC2 Type 2 Certified: Achieved through a comprehensive third-party audit, confirming Rent Manager's commitment to securely managing confidential and private information. Full SOC2 report available upon request at info.rentmanager.com/soc2-typeii.

NIST Cybersecurity Framework: Rent Manager's security strategy is built on NIST guidelines — the industry-leading federal cybersecurity framework.

TLS Encryption: All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher, protecting communications between systems.

Offsite Data Centers: Data is backed up in secure, access-controlled, camera-monitored offsite data centers with 24/7 monitoring and multiple daily backups.
